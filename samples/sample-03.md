# Sample 03 – Business Email Proofreading

## Original Email

Hi John,

Thank you for your email. I have recieve the documents yesterday and I will review it before Friday. If there is any questions I will let you know.

Best Regards,
Michael

---

## Errors Found

1. "recieve" → "received"
2. "review it" → "review them" (documents = plural)
3. "there is any questions" → "there are any questions"
4. "Best Regards" → "Best regards" (standard capitalization)

---

## Corrected Email

Hi John,

Thank you for your email. I received the documents yesterday, and I will review them before Friday. If there are any questions, I will let you know.

Best regards,

Michael
