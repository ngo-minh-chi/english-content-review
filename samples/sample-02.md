# Sample 02 – English Content Review

## Original Text

Artificial intelligence are changing healthcare by helping doctors make faster diagnosis and improving treatment plans. However, there is still concerns about patient privacy and ethical use of medical data.

---

## Issues Identified

1. "Artificial intelligence are" → should be **Artificial intelligence is**
2. "faster diagnosis" → should be **faster diagnoses**
3. "there is still concerns" → should be **there are still concerns**

---

## Corrected Version

Artificial intelligence is changing healthcare by helping doctors make faster diagnoses and improving treatment plans. However, there are still concerns about patient privacy and the ethical use of medical data.

---
