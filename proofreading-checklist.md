# Proofreading Checklist

## Before Reviewing

- Read the complete text.
- Understand the author's intended meaning.
- Identify the target audience.

---

## During Review

- Correct grammar mistakes.
- Correct spelling errors.
- Fix punctuation.
- Improve sentence clarity.
- Preserve the original meaning.
- Maintain formatting consistency.

---

## Final Check

- No grammar mistakes remain.
- Spelling is correct.
- Punctuation is consistent.
- Formatting follows the guideline.
- Text reads naturally.

---

## Review Standard

Every completed review should be:

✔ Accurate

✔ Consistent

✔ Professional

✔ Easy to read

✔ Faithful to the original content
